# Nilam
<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform
web

## Stack
Delegated: React map interface and Python API, explicitly accepted by the user. Python owns geospatial processing and model experiments.

## Users
People evaluating a location in Kerala for building a house. The creator also needs a technically substantial academic demonstration of training and explainability.

## Product Purpose
Select a location on a map in any of Kerala's 14 districts and inspect environmental and accessibility evidence, model estimates, explanations, and gaps in knowledge.

## Capabilities and Constraints
Public-data-first approach confirmed. Expert-reviewed suitability labels may become available later. Use existing local Earth Engine authentication and TabPFN 3.5 weights. Preserve reproducible experiments and changes in matgeorgie/nilam. Do not equate historical hazard susceptibility with permission to build. Soil bearing capacity, title, legal status, utility connections, crime, and affordability need evidence not currently provided. Native photography app is a proposed extension, not yet specified.

## Evidence on Hand
KSDMA historical flood layers in 14 district KMZ files and a landslide KML with low/medium/high classes. Source archives are locally retained with hashes; redistribution license is not established. Local southern-zone OpenStreetMap PBF and TabPFN weights are available. Earth Engine API connectivity is being checked. No expert suitability labels or validation results exist yet.

## Product Principles
- Evidence and predictions have distinct labels.
- Unknown values never silently become safe values.
- Evaluation must separate geographic areas and avoid target leakage.
- Every assessment exposes provenance and limitations.

## Brand Commitments
Nilam, matching the user's repository name. No visual style was prescribed.
